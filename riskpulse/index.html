Ask Risk

Answers use the loaded portfolio and deterministic calculations

No invented numbers
Your largest current risk is technology concentration. Ask me what drives it, which position contributes most, what happens under a market shock, or where liquidity is weakest.
01

Facts before explanations

Price, value, exposure, volume and liquidity are measured first. Narrative is shown separately as interpretation.

02

Survivable position size

Single-position loss is compared with a configurable risk budget. A thesis cannot be allowed to become captivity.

03

Liquidity under stress

Exit time is estimated from position size and participation in normal volume, with a stress multiplier for impaired markets.

04

Evidence can change the view

Limits and alerts update when portfolio evidence changes. No conclusion is permanent merely because it was previously accepted.

05

Counterparty visibility

Exposure is aggregated by broker or custodian so a portfolio does not mistake multiple positions for diversified operational risk.

06

Human responsibility

The system observes, calculates, stresses, alerts and explains. It does not execute, hedge, move funds or claim certainty.

Calculation notes

Transparent V1 methodology

Gross exposure is the sum of absolute market values divided by portfolio equity. Net exposure is signed market value divided by equity. Scenario loss is the sum of position value × shock × beta or sector sensitivity. Liquidity days use position shares divided by 10% of average daily volume. Concentration and counterparty limits are deterministic thresholds. This educational prototype does not calculate regulatory capital and is not investment advice.
